## Status and boundary

Image generation may remain useful for beauty or concept art, design-language reference, and optional non-authoritative appearance reference. It may not independently establish the geometry of multiple authority views.

## Next experiment: Shared Authority Geometry Scaffold

The experiment will create one coarse 3D geometry source and derive TOP, FRONT, and RIGHT authority images from that exact source using Blender orthographic cameras. The scaffold is not the final production mesh; it exists solely to provide coherent multiview reconstruction authority.

Requirements:

1. TOP remains the primary planform and identity anchor.
2. The scaffold represents separate major volumes: central fuselage, cockpit volume, left and right nacelles, dorsal weapon or equipment volume, forward gun volumes, and other silhouette-critical major components.
3. Beauty artwork is visual reference for vertical massing only and is not a geometric authority image.
4. A human operator may tune bounded height and profile parameters.
5. All three authority images are Blender renders from one geometry object or object set.
6. TOP, FRONT, and RIGHT cameras are exact orthographic cameras.
7. Camera transforms, orthographic scale, object origin, and render resolution are persisted in evidence.
8. The TOP render is compared back to the approved TOP authority using the project's existing silhouette-comparison discipline.
9. FRONT and RIGHT are derived from the same physical scaffold, preventing independent W/L/H proportion drift.
10. The MBS-196 deterministic cross-view consistency gate remains mandatory for scaffold-generated views.
11. No stochastic image generation occurs between scaffold rendering and authority-bundle import.

The experiment must retain human approval, exact file and bundle hashing, append-only lifecycle controls, stale-contract refusal, and all no-spend/provider boundaries. A new coherent bundle must pass the measured gate and receive human approval before MBS-195 can be considered for closure.
