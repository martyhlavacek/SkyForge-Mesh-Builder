# MBS-150 through MBS-154 Remediation Plan

- Alpha 1 structurally targets MBS-151 through MBS-154 with distinct fuselage, cockpit, engine, weapon, and recipe-governed underside volumes.
- MBS-150 remains open. Smooth-shell and contour-loft work is deferred to Alpha 2 after the multivolume architecture is independently measured.
- No finding is closed before independent review. Alpha 1 reports use “candidate addresses” only.
- VMP, UI, provider, and Sprite Foundry integration is deferred.
- No user testing is requested or authorized for Alpha 1.
- No paid-provider activity is authorized. MBS-136 is outside this experiment.

The Alpha 1 gunship recipe includes an explicit belly volume, but the experiment does not claim MBS-153 closure. The overlapping closed shells are intentionally not boolean-unioned.
